          About AgriVision AI 🌱

          Our Mission

          AgriVision AI aims to make crop health information easier to understand and access through technology.


          Project Features

          • Multiple crop categories.
          • Crop photograph upload.
          • Crop disease information.
          • Symptoms and severity information.
          • Organic and chemical management guidance.
          • Prevention practices.
          • Local scan history.
